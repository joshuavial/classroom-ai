# ADR-0011: Flags to classroom teacher only

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-07. Decided by: JV (D6).

## Context

Flags need to reach a person without adding infrastructure or an escalation process the product has to own.

## Decision

Flags appear in the teacher console only. No phone, push or email alerts, and no safeguarding contact. Self-harm shows the student a school-written message and is flagged to the classroom teacher, who marks it reviewed.

## Consequences

Follow-up is the teacher's call. No notification infrastructure to run.
